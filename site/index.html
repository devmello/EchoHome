For the Echo Show 5 (1st gen) running LineageOS

Your Echo,
without the clutter.

EchoHome replaces the stock home screen with clock faces, music, alarms and a voice assistant that run on the device itself. No Amazon account.

Set it up Latest release
Liquid Glass clock
EchoHome doesn't unlock your Echo. It needs an Echo Show 5, 1st generation (2019), that is already unlocked and running the unofficial LineageOS 18.1. Unlocking is covered in the LineageOS thread on XDA, and it can brick the device.

A home screen you read from across the room.

Everything is designed for a small screen on a nightstand or a kitchen counter: big type, soft glass, and nothing that asks for attention it doesn't need.

Clock faces

Clock faces, and a Night mode that stays out of your way.

Digital, Analog, World, Solar, Float and Liquid Glass, plus weather and calendar faces. Long-press to pick a face and its colour. At night every face turns dim red, so the screen doesn't light up the room.

  • Swipe to a Widgets page with weather, calendar, alarms, timers and air quality.
  • From across the room the clock grows and the details step back; walk up and they return.
Music

A speaker for whatever is already on your phone.

  • AirPlay from an iPhone, iPad or Mac (audio only).
  • Spotify Connect: the Echo shows up in the Spotify app (Premium needed).
  • Bluetooth with track info, cover art and controls.
  • Internet radio from the free Radio Browser directory.

What's playing lives in a small island at the top of the screen, with controls a tap away.

Bedside

Wake up gently. Fall asleep to rain.

  • Alarms with a sunrise ramp, snooze, and leave-by times from your calendar.
  • Sleep sounds made on the device: rain, ocean, fan, crickets and three noises, with a fading sleep timer.
  • "Good night" tells you when your alarm rings, turns on Do Not Disturb and Night mode, and starts your sound.
Glances

The morning in one look.

Weather, rain soon, air quality and UV, your next event and how long it takes to get there, without any API keys. A weather radar loops the last hour of rain over a map drawn on the device.

iPhone notifications and calls come in over Bluetooth, with no app on the phone.

Yours, and it stays on the device.

No Amazon account, no Home Assistant required, no cloud service in the middle.

Voice that listens locally

The wake word, speech-to-text and the spoken voice run on the Echo. Answers come from Claude with your own Anthropic API key.

A camera you can see working

Presence and optional face profiles run on the device. A label shows whenever the camera samples, and the shutter and mute button turn it off.

Settings on your phone

Scan a QR code on the Echo and set everything up from a web page on your home Wi-Fi, including which screens you want.

Only the screens you use

Hide and reorder pages. A hidden screen is never loaded, so it costs no memory, and it still opens by voice.

Updates from GitHub

New releases are checked nightly and verified before anything is installed. With the optional system install they install themselves while the room is empty, and roll back if one misbehaves.

Light on a 1 GB device

Plain Android views and pre-blurred glass keep it smooth on a 2019 chip. Home Assistant is optional, for lights, climate and scenes.

Already running LineageOS?

Setting up EchoHome is a few steps: turn on wireless debugging, install one file, pair your phone.
